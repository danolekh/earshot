---
title: Accessibility
description: What the parts do for keyboards, screen readers and reduced motion.
---

- **The scrubber** is one `role="slider"` with a spoken value ("0:12 of 1:11, Agent: …") and the
  keys on the [timeline page](/docs/timeline#the-scrubber). Waveforms, lanes, markers, the
  skimmer and the playhead are `aria-hidden`; the transcript carries the same events in words.
- **The transcript** is text. `Transcript.Seek` gives each turn one button ("Play from 0:12,
  Agent"), rather than a tab stop per word; clicking a word is the mouse's shortcut.
- **Live**, the root is a `role="log"` with `aria-busy` while a turn streams. Announcing finished
  turns is opt-in (`announce`), since a screen reader reading the transcript during a call talks
  over the agent.
- **Picking a moment:** the [finding list](/docs/inspector#findings) is one tab stop, the arrows
  moving between findings and `aria-current` on the pick. `Transcript.Pick` picks a turn from the
  keyboard. A bar that's a picture (`Latency.Bar`) is `aria-hidden` next to the same in words, and a
  word the recogniser missed reads as "not heard".
- **Keys:** every part that acts on a key calls `preventDefault()`, so your page's shortcuts can
  skip `event.defaultPrevented`. Single-letter shortcuts of your own need a way to turn them off
  (WCAG 2.1.4); the scrubber's only work while it has focus, which the rule allows.
- **The orb** is `aria-hidden` and says its state in a polite live region ("Listening").
- **Reduced motion:** the orb's phase stops and its state shows by brightness; visualizer bars hold
  still; the playhead jumps instead of gliding; following scrolls without smoothing. Nothing flashes
  more than three times a second.
- Every string is in `labels`: `EN_LABELS` and `DE_LABELS` ship, and `PlayerLabels` is the shape
  for more.

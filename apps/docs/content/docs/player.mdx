`Player.Root` holds the conversation and a clock. Everything inside follows it: the transcript
highlights along, the playhead moves, the orb can follow the recording.

```tsx
<Player.Root conversation={call} src="/calls/1042.mp3">
  <Player.Toggle />
  <Player.Time /> / <Player.Time show="duration" />
  <Player.Rate />
  {/* Transcript, Timeline, Orb … */}
</Player.Root>
```

Without `src` time runs on a virtual clock over the conversation's length, for a call you have
as text only. Pass `clock` to drive the parts from something else (a video element, a recorder).
The clock is read every frame by the parts that move, and nothing re-renders for it: coarse
state (the turn being played, the second shown) re-renders once when it changes.

`Player.Toggle` (a button named Play or Pause, `data-playing`), `Player.Time` (`show`: current,
duration or remaining) and `Player.Rate` (cycles 1, 1.5, 2) are the controls. `usePlayer()` and
`useClockValue(clock, select)` are there for your own.

## What's picked

The player also holds a selection: the turn, span and finding picked (`{ turnId, spanId,
findingId }`). The timeline's findings and spans, `Transcript.Pick` and the
[finding list](/docs/inspector#findings) set it; pass your own `selection` (from
`createSelection()`) to read or set it from outside, a URL say. `useSelected(selection, "turnId",
id)` re-renders a part only when its own pick changes.

## Listening to one side

For a stereo recording, `channels` says whose voice each channel carries (`["user", "agent"]`).
Then `Player.Mute` and `Player.Solo` (`speaker`: `user` or `agent`) mute or solo a side, as
`<button aria-pressed>` with `data-pressed`. With one side audible it plays in both ears. A
`Timeline.Lane` of a side you can't hear is `data-muted`, to dim it. The mix is built with Web
Audio on the first press, inside the click, as browsers require.

```tsx
<Player.Root conversation={call} src="/call.ogg" channels={["user", "agent"]}>
  <Player.Mute speaker="user" /> <Player.Solo speaker="user" />
</Player.Root>
```

`useListening(speaker)` gives `{ muted, soloed, heard }` for your own controls.

One orb: a sphere of drifting sky behind glass. Its outline is an exact circle that only ever
scales; everything it has to say happens inside it and in its light.

- **Clouds** are noise sampled on the sphere's own surface and turned slowly, so they wrap round
  it like weather on a globe, over a sky that runs from the palette's top colour to its deep one.
- **Glass**: the inside is seen through a dome-shaped lens (the displacement field from Aave's
  [Building Glass for the Web](https://aave.com/design/building-glass-for-the-web)), so it
  magnifies toward the rim, with a little colour fringing there, a fresnel edge and a soft
  highlight.
- **A halo** hugs the sphere and fades to nothing within a sixth of its radius: round by
  construction, so it never shows the canvas's square.

## States

`state` is `idle`, `listening`, `thinking` or `speaking`, and the orb eases between them over
about half a second, once a new state has held for a quarter second.

|           | Size                               | Flow                     | Light                                               | Moved by                     |
| --------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| idle      | breathes, ±1.5 % over 4 s          | a slow drift             | even                                                | nothing                      |
| listening | swells with you, up to 3.5 %       | livelier                 | a little brighter                                   | the caller's voice (`input`) |
| thinking  | breathes quicker                   | a steady churn           | a soft light orbits the globe; the sky leans violet | a clock, never audio         |
| speaking  | swells with the voice, up to 5.5 % | churns on loud syllables | lit softly from within                              | the agent's voice (`output`) |

A voice goes through an envelope follower, quick to rise (25 ms) and slower to fall (180 ms), so
syllables read as rhythm rather than jitter; that drives a spring on the scale that's under
critically damped, which gives the soft bounce. Each syllable's onset kicks the spring and flashes
the light inside the sphere; loud stretches make the clouds boil (two layers, at different depths,
sliding past each other); the voice's brightness warms the light a touch and its hiss twinkles in
the glass. The flow and the sphere's turn are integrated as phases, so they speed up without ever
jumping.

`liveliness` on `Orb.Root` scales all of it: 0.5 is calm, 1 the default, 2 vivid.

## Colours

```tsx
<Orb.Shader
  params={{ colors: ["#fff3e6", "#ffa25c", "#c2410c"], thinkingColor: "#8a7cff", glass: 0.6, halo: 0.5 }}
/>
```

`colors` is the sky's top, middle and deep colour; `thinkingColor` the tint thinking leans
toward; `glass` how strongly the lens bends and fringes (0 to 1); `halo` the soft light around it
(0 to 1, 0 for none).

## Without WebGL

`Orb.Root` sets `data-state` and, every frame, `--orb-level` and `--orb-idle`, `--orb-listening`,
`--orb-thinking`, `--orb-speaking` (each 0 to 1, easing). A CSS-only orb, or the fallback before
the shader's first frame, reads those:

```css
[data-slot="orb"] {
  background: radial-gradient(circle at 40% 30%, #eef6ff, #1553c9 75%);
  border-radius: 50%;
  transform: scale(calc(1 + var(--orb-level) * 0.05));
}
```

`--orb-bleed` on the root gives the canvas room for the halo past the orb's edge. For your own
shader, pass `definition`: it reads the driver's `uScale`, `uPhase`, `uBright`, `uAura`, `uThink`,
`uHue`, `uLevel` and the state weights as uniforms. Under reduced motion the orb holds still and
shows its state by brightness and tint; the canvas is `aria-hidden` and the state is
announced in words.
